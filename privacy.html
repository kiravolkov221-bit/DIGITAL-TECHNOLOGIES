Works Fully Offline No Account Required No Ads or Tracking

Overview

DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES is designed to work fully offline. No account is required.

We do not collect or share personal data. Your app content stays on your device unless you choose to export notes.

Data Handling

  • The app does not collect personal data.
  • The app does not transmit user data over the internet.
  • The app does not share data with third parties.
  • The app does not include ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • The app does not request any dangerous runtime permissions from users.

What Is Stored Locally

The following content is stored only on your device:

  • Saved notes
  • Checklist selections
  • Favorite guide topics

Exports

You can export notes as .txt files using the Android system file picker. Export is optional and initiated only by the user.

  • Exported files are saved in the location you choose.
  • Exported files are outside app storage and are not removed by app uninstall.

Retention and Deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it.

  • Delete individual notes from the Notes screen.
  • Reset checklist items from the Checklist screen.
  • Remove favorites from topic screens or the Favorites screen.
  • With backup disabled, uninstalling the app removes local app storage data from this device. Exported .txt files are not removed.
