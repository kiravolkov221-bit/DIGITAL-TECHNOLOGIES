Privacy Policy

App: DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES
Developer: DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES
Effective Date: February 25, 2026

Overview

DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES is designed to operate fully offline. No account is required to use this application.

Data Handling

  • The app does not request any runtime permissions from users.
  • No personal data is collected.
  • No user data is transmitted over the internet.
  • No data is shared with third parties.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs are included.

Local Storage

The following data may be stored locally on your device:

  • Saved notes
  • Checklist selections
  • Favorite guide topics
All stored information remains on your device only.

Exports

You may export notes as .txt files using the Android system file picker. Export is optional and initiated only by you.

  • Exported files are saved in the location you choose.
  • Exported files are outside the app’s storage and are not removed upon app uninstall.

Retention & Deletion

  • You can delete notes inside the app.
  • You can reset checklist selections.
  • You can remove saved favorites.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the app’s local storage from your device.
